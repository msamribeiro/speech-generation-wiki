## Overview

Two 2020 arXiv preprints anchor the non-autoregressive and GAN vocoder foundations of the field. [[2006.04558]] (FastSpeech 2) eliminated teacher-student distillation from non-autoregressive TTS and introduced the variance adaptor (pitch, energy, duration) that defined the enc-dec TTS template for the subsequent years. [[2010.05646]] (HiFi-GAN) resolved the quality gap between GAN vocoders and autoregressive models by introducing the multi-period discriminator, achieving MOS above WaveNet at 167x real-time — making it the vocoder of choice for the subsequent generation of acoustic model research.
