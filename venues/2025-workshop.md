# Workshop Papers 2025

## Overview

Workshop papers 2025 includes 4 papers from diverse venues. Key themes: (1) low-resource and under-resourced language TTS — [[2025.americasnlp-1.1]] targets Shipibo-Konibo (a Peruvian Panoan language) and [[2025.ccl-1.80]] extends VALL-E X to Lao-English code-switching without bilingual training data; (2) prosody modeling — DiffStyleTTS ([[2025.coling-main.352]]) uses hierarchical conditional diffusion for prosody control (MOS 4.18 Mandarin), while ProsodyFlow ([[2025.coling-main.518]]) applies conditional flow matching specifically to a WavLM-extracted prosody latent space, achieving MOS 4.23 on LJSpeech (near-human-level 4.25) — the first application of FM to prosody latent generation. The workshop collection highlights the breadth of speech synthesis research beyond mainstream English TTS.
