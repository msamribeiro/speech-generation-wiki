# ASRU 2025

## Overview

ASRU (IEEE Automatic Speech Recognition and Understanding Workshop) 2025 is currently represented by three papers in the corpus spanning codec tokenization analysis, automatic audio quality assessment, and controllable accented TTS. [[2508.08957]] (QAMRO) contributes a quality-aware pairwise ranking optimization for automatic MOS prediction, demonstrating that incorporating ranking structure into MOS predictor training improves system-level correlation across TTS, TTM, and TTA domains. [[2508.11224]] provides a controlled empirical benchmark for prosody encoding in SSL-derived discrete speech tokens. [[2508.07426]] investigates scalable accented TTS from geographically-collected data.

[[2508.07426]] (Scalable Controllable Accented TTS) investigates whether fine-tuning a multilingual TTS backbone (XTTS-v2) on geographically diverse, automatically collected accent data produces perceptually distinct and intelligible accented speech. The paper contributes a scalable data collection pipeline using geographic bounding boxes to harvest accent-labelled audio without manual annotation, covering 77 English accent regions. The main finding is that fine-tuning improves accent similarity (cosine similarity 0.78–0.89) and naturalness on automated metrics, though human evaluation shows mixed results — suggesting that automatic speaker-similarity metrics may not fully capture perceptual accent distinctiveness.
