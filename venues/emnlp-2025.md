# EMNLP 2025

## Overview

EMNLP 2025 contributes 4 papers across SCA, TTS, and evaluation. The dominant theme is **spoken conversational intelligence**: VocalNet ([[2025.emnlp-main.989]]) demonstrates that multi-token prediction (MTP) halves WER in speech LLMs while matching large-scale models with far less training data; OpenS2S ([[2025.emnlp-demos.70]]) provides the first fully open-source empathetic end-to-end speech dialogue system. FillerSpeech ([[2025.emnlp-main.1730]]) addresses paralinguistic naturalness through filler word insertion, and InteractSpeech ([[2025.findings-emnlp.424]]) provides interaction-annotated dialogue data for duplex modeling. EMNLP 2025 reflects the NLP community's growing focus on speech as a first-class modality for language models, with particular emphasis on empathetic interaction and data-efficient training.
