# ACL 2025

## Overview

ACL 2025 is the largest venue in the corpus by paper count (13 papers), spanning TTS, VC, SCA, and evaluation. Four dominant themes emerge. First, **flow-matching TTS maturity**: F5-TTS ([[2410.06885]]) establishes non-autoregressive flow matching with DiT+ConvNeXt V2 as the open-weight zero-shot TTS reference, while [[2025.acl-long.313]] (previous arXiv version) and OZSpeech ([[2025.acl-long.1043]]) further advance this paradigm. Second, **bypassing vector quantization**: MELLE ([[2025.acl-long.65]]) demonstrates that autoregressive prediction of continuous mel-spectrogram frames outperforms VQ-based codec language models on both quality and robustness, providing the clearest empirical argument against mandatory discretization. Third, **codec reliability**: [[2025.acl-long.1498]] characterizes and mitigates the Discrete Representation Inconsistency problem in RVQ codecs. Fourth, **speech language model breadth**: [[2025.acl-long.682]] provides the field's most comprehensive SpeechLM taxonomy, and [[2025.acl-long.388]] (DiVA) demonstrates cross-modal distillation as a training-data-efficient route to instruction-following speech models. The venue reflects NLP community engagement with speech synthesis as a core LLM capability.
