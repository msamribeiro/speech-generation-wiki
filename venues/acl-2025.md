# ACL 2025

## Overview

ACL 2025 is the largest venue in the corpus by paper count (11 papers), spanning TTS, VC, SCA, and evaluation. Three dominant themes emerge. First, **flow-matching TTS maturity**: F5-TTS ([[2025.acl-long.313]]) establishes non-autoregressive flow matching as the open-weight zero-shot TTS reference, while OZSpeech ([[2025.acl-long.1043]]) demonstrates single-step inference via learned-prior OT-CFM, and [[2025.acl-long.598]] introduces DPO-FM for preference-aligning flow-matching models. Second, **codec reliability**: [[2025.acl-long.1498]] characterizes and mitigates the Discrete Representation Inconsistency problem in RVQ codecs, directly improving downstream VALL-E quality. Third, **speech language model breadth**: [[2025.acl-long.682]] provides the field's most comprehensive SpeechLM taxonomy, and [[2025.acl-long.388]] (DiVA) demonstrates cross-modal distillation as a training-data-efficient route to instruction-following speech models. The venue reflects NLP community engagement with speech synthesis as a core LLM capability — all standout papers frame TTS/SCA problems within LLM or preference-optimization paradigms.
