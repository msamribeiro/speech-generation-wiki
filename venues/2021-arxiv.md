## Overview

The two arXiv 2021 papers in this corpus represent complementary infrastructure contributions. [[2106.15561]] (A Survey on Neural Speech Synthesis) provides a comprehensive taxonomy of neural TTS up to mid-2021, covering acoustic models, vocoders, and expressivity approaches across 100+ systems. It synthesises the state of the art at the inflection point between the transformer encoder-decoder era (FastSpeech, Tacotron family) and the emerging diffusion and flow-based generation methods, while predating the codec LM paradigm entirely. [[2106.06909]] (GigaSpeech) introduces a 10,000-hour multi-domain ASR corpus that became a standard training and evaluation resource across ASR and TTS work. Together these papers mark the field's maturation point: systematic review became necessary as the number of TTS approaches proliferated, and large-scale diverse data infrastructure was needed to move beyond audiobook-centric evaluation.
