# NAACL 2025

## Overview

NAACL 2025 is represented by one paper in the corpus: [[2025.findings-naacl.184]] (Continuous Speech Tokenizer in Text To Speech). It challenges the assumption that residual vector quantization is necessary in codec-based TTS, proposing a continuous speech tokenizer that retains encoder outputs directly rather than discretizing them. The paper demonstrates measurable high-frequency information retention advantages and improved downstream TTS quality (WER 6.59% vs. 12.73% for VALL-E on LibriSpeech).
