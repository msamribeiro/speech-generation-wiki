# ACL 2025

## Overview

ACL 2025 is the largest venue in the corpus by paper count (18 papers), spanning TTS, VC, SCA, evaluation, and codec design. Five dominant themes emerge. First, **flow-matching TTS maturity**: F5-TTS ([[2410.06885]]) establishes non-autoregressive flow matching with DiT+ConvNeXt V2 as the open-weight zero-shot TTS reference, while OZSpeech ([[2025.acl-long.1043]]) introduces single-step inference via learned-prior flow matching. Second, **bypassing vector quantization**: MELLE ([[2025.acl-long.65]]) demonstrates that autoregressive prediction of continuous mel-spectrogram frames outperforms VQ-based codec language models; Language-Codec ([[2025.acl-long.654]]) addresses this from the codec design side via MCRVQ redistribution. Third, **codec reliability and design for LM**: [[2025.acl-long.1498]] characterizes and mitigates Discrete Representation Inconsistency in RVQ codecs; Language-Codec ([[2025.acl-long.654]]) introduces a codec designed to lower AR LM prediction entropy. Fourth, **speech language model breadth**: [[2025.acl-long.682]] provides the field's most comprehensive SpeechLM taxonomy; [[2025.acl-long.388]] (DiVA) demonstrates cross-modal distillation; LLaMA-Omni 2 ([[2025.acl-long.912]]) demonstrates that modular SpeechLMs can match native SpeechLMs at 200K synthetic samples. Fifth, **controllability and evaluation**: [[2025.acl-long.346]] (ControlSpeech) and [[2025.acl-long.598]] (preference alignment) advance multi-factor control; DNASpeech ([[2025.acl-long.911]]) introduces situated-context TTS evaluation; Vietnamese zero-shot TTS ([[2025.acl-short.81]]) documents low-resource codec LM adaptation. The venue reflects strong NLP community engagement with speech synthesis as a core LLM capability.
