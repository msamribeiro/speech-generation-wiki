# arXiv 2025

## Overview

This page tracks arXiv preprints ingested into the corpus. Ten papers are currently represented (six ingested in the previous session; four added 2026-05-28). Two are confirmed accepted to top venues: [[2510.00981]] (FlexiCodec → ICLR 2026) and [[2507.14534]] (Conan → ASRU 2025). [[2412.17048]] is accepted to ICASSP 2026. [[2502.03930]] (DiTAR) is accepted to ICML 2025. [[2504.10352]] (PALLE) is accepted to ACM MM 2025. The remaining papers are preprints ranging from 2023 to 2025.

The arXiv 2025 corpus spans the full range of tasks: TTS (majority), VC ([[2507.14534]]), codec ([[2510.00981]]), and SCA ([[2412.17048]], [[2509.02020]]). Dominant themes in the newly added papers: (1) hybrid AR+diffusion for continuous speech generation without discrete tokenization ([[2502.03930]] DiTAR, with 3–43x lower FLOPs than NAR diffusion); (2) new decoding paradigms unifying AR and NAR: pseudo-autoregressive (PAR) codec LM ([[2504.10352]] PALLE achieves 10x speedup over AR with state-of-the-art quality on LibriTTS); (3) inference acceleration for LLM-based TTS via multi-token prediction and verification ([[2508.06262]] Llasa+, 1.48x speedup with frozen backbone); (4) production-level TTS engineering with GRPO multi-reward RL ([[2512.14291]] GLM-TTS).
