# EMNLP 2025

## Overview

EMNLP 2025 contributes 8 papers across SCA, TTS, evaluation, and post-training. The dominant theme is **spoken conversational intelligence**: VocalNet ([[2025.emnlp-main.989]]) demonstrates that multi-token prediction (MTP) halves WER in speech LLMs while matching large-scale models with far less training data; OpenS2S ([[2025.emnlp-demos.70]]) provides the first fully open-source empathetic end-to-end speech dialogue system. FillerSpeech ([[2025.emnlp-main.1730]]) addresses paralinguistic naturalness through filler word insertion, and InteractSpeech ([[2025.findings-emnlp.424]]) provides interaction-annotated dialogue data for duplex modeling. Two new TTS papers extend the venue's coverage: GOAT ([[2508.15442]]) is the first application of GFlowNets to LM-based TTS for hallucination reduction, achieving >50% CER reduction on hard subsets; VoiceCraft-X ([[2511.12347]]) extends the VoiceCraft codec LM to 11 languages via Qwen3 backbone with the highest reported CMOS (0.63) among compared systems on Seed-TTS test-en. ParaSpeechCaps ([[2025.emnlp-main.180]]) is the first large-scale (2709h) open-source dataset with 59 rich paralinguistic style tags for instruction-conditioned TTS, including an automatic scaling pipeline using Gemini 2.0 Flash as an audio LLM quality filter. EMNLP 2025 reflects the NLP community's growing focus on speech as a first-class modality for language models, with particular emphasis on empathetic interaction, data-efficient training, and scalable evaluation and annotation.
