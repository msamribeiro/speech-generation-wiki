## Context in Speech Generation

DNSMOS is a non-intrusive (no clean reference needed) speech quality predictor: a small CNN over 120-band log-power Mel spectrograms of 9-second, 16 kHz clips, regressed onto P.808 MOS labels for about 120,000 clips produced by over 200 noise suppression methods *(§2, §3.2, Table 1)*. Because P.808 per-clip ratings are noisy, the authors train successive self-teaching student models on a weighted mix of human ratings and earlier model predictions, which raises per-system rank correlation with human MOS on the ICASSP 2021 DNS Challenge blind set from 0.65 to 0.9 and improves generalization to non-English, tonal, emotional and singing speech unseen in training *(§3.1, Table 3)*. On the Interspeech DNS challenge results, the primary model correlates with averaged human ratings better than PESQ, POLQA and SDR (PCC 0.93, SRCC 0.94) *(Table 2)*. Since it needs no reference signal, it can score generated or in-the-wild speech directly, which makes it usable as an automatic quality metric for synthesized speech and as a quality filter when curating large speech training corpora; note that it was designed and validated for ranking noise suppressors, not TTS systems.
