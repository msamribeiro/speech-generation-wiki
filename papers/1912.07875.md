## Context in Speech Generation

Libri-Light is built from English LibriVox audiobooks converted to 16 kHz FLAC, with files of unknown or multiple speakers removed and LibriSpeech dev and test speakers excluded; its largest split (unlab-60k) holds 57,706.4 hours from 7,439 speakers, with nested unlab-6k and unlab-600 cuts matched in genre distribution (Table 1). Each file carries JSON metadata with a unique speaker ID, average SNR, genre and voice-activity segments, which makes the corpus usable as a large multi-speaker read-speech resource beyond ASR. The paper also defines 10 h, 1 h and 10 min labelled subsets of LibriSpeech and evaluates three settings (ABX for unsupervised representations, PER/CER for semi-supervised, WER for distant supervision) on the standard LibriSpeech dev and test sets, using a CPC baseline whose ABX and WER improve as unlabelled pre-training data grows (Tables 2 to 4). For speech generation work, it provides a large untranscribed, speaker-labelled read-speech corpus that can serve as training data for self-supervised speech encoders and for generative models trained on audio alone or on pseudo-labelled transcripts.
