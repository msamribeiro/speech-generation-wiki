## Context in Speech Generation

Conformer replaces the Transformer block in a speech encoder with a four-module block: a half-step feed-forward module, multi-headed self-attention with relative sinusoidal positional encoding, a convolution module (pointwise convolution with a gated linear unit, a 1-D depthwise convolution and batchnorm), and a second half-step feed-forward module followed by layer normalization (§2). In a transducer ASR system with a single-layer LSTM decoder, the 118.8M-parameter Conformer(L) reaches 2.1%/4.3% WER on LibriSpeech test-clean/test-other without a language model and 1.9%/3.9% with an external LSTM language model, while the 10.3M-parameter Conformer(S) reaches 2.7%/6.3% without one (Table 2). Ablations identify the convolution module as the most important difference from a Transformer block, with the Macaron-style feed-forward pair also helping (§3.4.1). The block is a general sequence encoder for frame-rate speech features, so it can serve as an encoder or backbone in speech generation systems (for text, speech-prompt or token sequences) as well as in the ASR models used to score the intelligibility of synthesized speech.
