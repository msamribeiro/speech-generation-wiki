## Context in Speech Generation

The paper introduces scaled dot-product attention, multi-head attention, sinusoidal positional encodings and a stack of residual self-attention and feed-forward blocks with layer normalization, arranged as a 6-layer encoder and a 6-layer decoder whose self-attention is masked to preserve autoregressive generation *(§3)*. Because a self-attention layer connects all positions with a constant number of sequential operations, unlike the O(n) operations of a recurrent layer, the design allows far more parallel training over long sequences *(§4, Table 1)*, a property that matters for frame-level acoustic sequences in TTS. Encoder-decoder attention lets every decoder position attend over the whole input, which provides the text-to-acoustic alignment mechanism that attention-based acoustic models build on, while the masked decoder stack can serve as the backbone of autoregressive speech-token language models. The paper itself evaluates only on text tasks: 28.4 BLEU on WMT 2014 English-to-German and 41.8 BLEU on English-to-French with the big model, plus English constituency parsing *(§6, Table 2)*.
