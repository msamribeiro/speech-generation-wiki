## Context in Speech Generation

FLEURS takes the 2,009 publicly available dev and devtest sentences of FLoRes-101, re-splits them into 1,509 train, 150 dev and 350 test sentences, and collects up to three validated recordings per sentence from native speakers in each of 102 languages, at 16 kHz and with speakers disjoint between train/dev and test (§2.1). Because sentence indices are shared across languages, the same content is available as speech and text in every language, covering 17 language families and 27 writing systems (§2.3), and the release includes raw, normalized and character-tokenized transcripts plus the English translations (§2.2). This parallel, many-language design provides a common multilingual test bed for ASR, speech-to-text and speech-to-speech translation, language identification and cross-modal retrieval (§1), which also makes it usable as an evaluation source for multilingual TTS intelligibility, speech translation and the speech recognition abilities of speech LMs. The paper's own baselines fine-tune 600M-parameter w2v-bert-51 and mSLAM models and report per-region CER and LangID accuracy, with the speech-only model slightly ahead on ASR and mSLAM ahead on LangID (73.3% vs. 71.4% macro-average accuracy, §4.2).
