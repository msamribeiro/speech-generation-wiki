## Method-Family Evidence

### Native joint speech-text token language models

A single autoregressive decoder generates interleaved or parallel speech and text tokens directly from an LLM-derived vocabulary, without a separate downstream TTS module. This is the lineage running from SpeechGPT through Moshi and OmniFlatten to their multilingual, reasoning, and duplex-DST descendants.

### Dual-stream / asymmetric full-duplex architectures

User and agent audio are routed through separate pathways, a pretrained streaming encoder for input and a distinct codec/decoder for output, rather than flattened into a single token stream, allowing full-duplex interaction without requiring the backbone LLM to learn speech representations end-to-end.

- Whether asymmetric duplex designs scale to open-domain, high-complexity reasoning tasks as well as the flattened single-stream alternative.
- How text-insertion timing and chunking policies generalize across languages with different morphological density.

### Modular LLM with gated / aligned streaming TTS decoder

The backbone text LLM is kept largely intact (frozen or lightly adapted) and a separately-trained streaming autoregressive or flow-matching TTS decoder is conditioned on its hidden states or text tokens via learned fusion (gating, cross-attention, or CTC), rather than training a single joint token stream from scratch.
